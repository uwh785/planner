# Product

## Register

product

## Users
People who keep a personal list of tasks and reminders and want it with them everywhere, including places with no connection (commute, basement classroom, airplane mode). Each person creates their own account and signs in; there is no shared workspace. They open the app in short moments: capture a task, check what is next, tick something off, start or pause a timer.

## Product Purpose
A personal task and reminder planner for mobile that works fully offline and syncs with the existing Planner backend when a connection returns. Tasks, lists, tags, subtasks, classes and events stay readable and editable without a network; changes made offline are uploaded later without loss or duplication. Success is capturing and completing tasks with no friction, and never having to wonder whether the app needs internet.

## Brand Personality
Calm, minimal, light. The interface should lower the user's stress, not add to it: quiet surfaces, generous space, restrained color, and gentle reminders. Voice is plain and short.

## Anti-references
Dense, data-heavy apps in the style of Binance: crowded screens, many numbers and charts, heavy chrome, constant visual noise. Nothing should feel like a dashboard to monitor.

## Design Principles
1. Calm over dense: show less, one main thing per screen, and let empty space do work.
2. Offline is the normal state: every screen behaves the same with or without a connection; sync status is present but discreet, never a blocking alert.
3. Capture in seconds: creating and completing a task takes the fewest possible taps.
4. Reminders inform without shouting: notifications and overdue states are clear and polite, not alarming.
5. Accessible by default: it is a baseline, not a later pass.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA as the baseline: text contrast of at least 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text), touch targets large enough for comfortable use, text that respects the system font size setting, and reduced motion honored when the user asks for it. No information conveyed by color alone (overdue, priority, sync state also use text or icon).
